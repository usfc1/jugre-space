# JUGRE Parts

Gameplay specifications for the construction catalog. The set has 25 part types and 26 GLB models. The left and right fairing shells are separate models.

Mesh dimensions are listed as X × Y × Z. Attachment diameters are nominal. Thrust and specific impulse are listed in sea-level / vacuum order.
